Story Rewrite · Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Story Rewrite handles when you visit the website, create an account, play, use multiplayer and social features, ask StoryBot for help, use text-to-speech, or connect a YouTube account.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. About this policy and how to contact us

“Story Rewrite” means the online game and related features available at storyrewritegame.com. This policy describes the service's current features and data practices. It may be updated when the service changes.

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the Story Rewrite team at ilikegamingalot3329@gmail.com.

2. Information Story Rewrite collects or processes

Account information

  • Your Story Rewrite username and account creation information.
  • Your password in the form of a salted, one-way scrypt hash. The account system does not store your password as readable text.
  • Authentication and session information used to sign you in and keep your account secure.

Creating a standard Story Rewrite account asks for a username and password; it does not require you to enter your real name, phone number, or email address.

Game progress and settings

We store game-save information needed to sync your progress, such as unlocked worlds and stages, your current stage, stars, lives, deaths, and game settings such as music and sound volume.

Multiplayer and social features

When you use multiplayer or social features, the service may process your username, room participation, player status, presence or last-seen information, direct messages, message reactions, and information needed to deliver and display conversations.

Messages submitted through reporting or moderation tools may be reviewed with their surrounding context. A moderation report can include the reported message, a snapshot of its text, the reporting and reported usernames, and the reason supplied with the report. Direct messages are stored so the conversation can be delivered and shown to its participants. Room activity and room chat may be handled temporarily while a room is active.

Browser and technical information

Story Rewrite uses browser storage, including local storage and session storage, for authentication/session information, remembered account usernames, and local profile or game-state snapshots. The saved-account list stores usernames and usage times, not passwords.

When requests reach the service, our hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser or device details, and error information. The backend derives a one-way hash from the connecting network address for login-abuse throttling. Hosting providers may also maintain standard operational and security logs.

YouTube connection information

If you choose Connect YouTube, Google handles the authorization step. The connection requests basic Google identity scopes (openid, email, and profile) together with the YouTube read-only scope. The current Story Rewrite integration uses the authorized access to look up the connected channel and stores that channel's YouTube channel ID, channel title, connection timestamps, and an encrypted OAuth refresh token. It does not currently store your Google email or profile attributes, and it does not store your Google password.

3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate sessions, and prevent unauthorized access or repeated login abuse.
  • Save and synchronize game progress, settings, multiplayer rooms, and social features.
  • Deliver direct messages, display player availability, and operate room participation.
  • Review user reports, enforce the community rules, investigate abuse, and protect players and the service.
  • Connect and display the YouTube channel you chose to link.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain service reliability, and respond to support requests.

4. StoryBot, AI moderation, and text-to-speech

When you ask StoryBot a question, the service sends the question and relevant game context to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Depending on the active room and the question, that context can include the room or stage details, player usernames, game state, and recent room-chat messages.

For AI-assisted moderation, the reported message and up to 12 recent conversation messages may be sent to Google's Gemini service for contextual review. This helps assess a report in context; a report is not treated as proof of a violation by itself.

When you use text-to-speech, the text selected for speech generation is sent to the speech service configured for that deployment. The backend supports xAI text-to-speech when configured and an Edge TTS fallback. This feature processes text to generate audio; it is not a feature for recording your microphone.

Important: Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, payment details, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in StoryBot prompts, stories, or chat messages.

5. Google API and YouTube data

The YouTube connection is optional. When you approve it, Story Rewrite uses the YouTube read-only permission to retrieve the channel ID and channel title for the channel associated with the Google account you selected. The current integration does not upload, publish, edit, or delete videos, and does not access or change channel content for those purposes.

The refresh token is encrypted on the server using AES-256-GCM and is used to maintain the connection within the permissions you granted. The current integration stores the channel ID and title plus that encrypted refresh token; it does not store your Google password. The access token is used to retrieve the channel details and is not saved as the connection record.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will be limited to providing and maintaining the YouTube connection described above, securing that feature, and complying with applicable legal requirements. It will not be sold or used for advertising. Our handling of Google API data is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect YouTube in Story Rewrite Settings. Disconnecting removes the stored connection from Story Rewrite. To revoke Google's authorization as well, visit your Google Account permissions.

Google's own handling of your information is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to operate the features you use, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

  • Other players: Your username, player/presence information, room activity, and messages are visible to the people or rooms involved in the relevant multiplayer or social feature. A direct message is delivered to its intended conversation participant(s).
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: The website is hosted through Vercel and the backend runs through Render. The backend uses a PostgreSQL database service to store account, save, social, report, and YouTube-connection records. These providers process information as needed to host, secure, deliver, and maintain the service.
  • Google: Google processes information when you use Google Gemini features or authorize the optional YouTube connection, as described in this policy.
  • Speech service providers: The configured text-to-speech provider processes text submitted for audio generation.
  • Safety and legal matters: Information may be reviewed by authorized service moderators or disclosed where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, enforce the Terms of Service, comply with law, or respond to valid legal requests.

Providers may process information in locations outside your province, state, or country. The privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you live.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep account and game-save information while it is needed to provide your account and the service. Direct messages, reactions, reports, and moderation records are retained for as long as needed to provide conversations, review reports, protect players, resolve disputes, and maintain service security.

You can delete messages using available in-app controls, and you can disconnect YouTube in Settings. Deleted messages or accounts may remain in limited moderation, security, legal, or backup records for a period where necessary. Backups and operational logs may also follow the retention schedules of the infrastructure providers.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, email ilikegamingalot3329@gmail.com from an address where we can safely follow up. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law allows or requires it, or where it is necessary for safety and security.

8. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including salted one-way password hashing, encrypted storage of YouTube refresh tokens, authenticated account checks, and security controls intended to limit abuse. No website, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

9. Your choices

  • You can choose not to connect YouTube or use optional AI and text-to-speech features.
  • You can disconnect YouTube from Story Rewrite Settings and separately revoke the app's access through your Google Account.
  • You can manage information through available in-app controls and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.
  • You can clear Story Rewrite's local browser storage, although doing so may sign you out and remove locally remembered usernames or cached snapshots.

10. Children

Do not use Story Rewrite to submit another person's personal information or sensitive details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Story Rewrite in a way that should not have happened, contact us at ilikegamingalot3329@gmail.com so we can review the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Story Rewrite's features, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when the current version was published. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
