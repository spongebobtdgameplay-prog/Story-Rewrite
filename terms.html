Story Rewrite · Multiplayer

Terms of Service

Story Rewrite is a shared story game. Keep multiplayer chat welcoming, play fairly, and report harmful messages instead of escalating the situation. These rules apply to chat, usernames, room activity, and other player-facing content.

Community rules · Applies to multiplayer features

1. Be respectful

Do not use multiplayer features to swear at people or make the game hostile for them. Keep disagreements about the game focused on the game, not on attacking a player.

  • No profanity or obscene language in ordinary multiplayer chat.
  • No slurs, hateful attacks, discriminatory abuse, personal insults, bullying, or targeted harassment.
  • No sexual harassment, threats, intimidation, or encouragement of self-harm or violence.
  • No sharing another person's private information, impersonation, spam, or attempts to evade moderation.

2. Story content and context

Stories can contain fictional danger, villains, peril, and other dramatic events. Fictional story content is not automatically a real-world threat. Moderation should consider what a message means, who it targets, and the conversation around it.

Context matters: quoting a message to ask for help or report abuse is different from using those words to attack another player. Do not use quotation or roleplay as a pretext for harassing real players.

3. Reporting harmful messages

Use the Report control attached to a player's chat message when you believe it breaks these rules. Reports are reviewed against the message and available surrounding conversation. A report is a request for review, not proof that a violation occurred.

  • Report the message that contains the concern and avoid retaliation or public pile-ons.
  • Do not knowingly submit false, misleading, or retaliatory reports.
  • StoryBot can explain the rules and help you think through a situation, but it does not independently file a report or promise a moderation outcome.

4. Moderation and consequences

Moderation may use automated checks and AI-assisted review of reported or flagged messages. Simple keyword matches are signals to review content, not conclusive proof on their own. Review considers the exact message, surrounding context, and these Terms.

Depending on the content, severity, and repetition, actions may include censoring or removing a message, a warning, a temporary chat timeout, removal from a room, or further restrictions. Attempts to evade a restriction can lead to additional action.

5. Protect your information

Do not post passwords, authentication tokens, addresses, phone numbers, financial details, or other sensitive information about yourself or someone else. If a message exposes personal information, report it and avoid reposting it.

6. Agreement and updates

By using Story Rewrite multiplayer features, you agree to follow these rules and any in-game moderation instructions. The rules may be updated as the game evolves. Continued use after an update means you are expected to follow the current version.

If something is unclear, ask for clarification respectfully or use the game's reporting tools. Do not use the rules as a reason to target or shame another player.
These community rules explain expected behaviour in Story Rewrite; they are not legal advice and do not create a guarantee that every automated review will be correct.
