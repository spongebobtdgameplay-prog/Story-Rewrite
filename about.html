Story Rewrite · About

Change the story.

Story Rewrite is an interactive story-puzzle game where the next part of the story is not always fixed. Players explore different worlds, face survival challenges, and rewrite key sentences to change what happens next.

How it works

Each challenge gives you a scene and a set of story sentences. Choose a sentence to cross out, write a replacement, and see how your change affects the situation. The goal is to think creatively, understand the rules of the scene, and find a way forward.

  • Explore five story worlds and a range of survival-puzzle challenges.
  • Unlock stages, earn stars, and keep your progress saved to your account.
  • Play on your own or join multiplayer rooms with other players.

More than a puzzle

Story Rewrite also includes player accounts, multiplayer chat, social features, and optional StoryBot help. Some optional features use third-party services, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Players are expected to treat one another respectfully. Read the Terms of Service for the community rules that apply to multiplayer chat and other player-facing features.
