Rules

Story Rewrite rules

Every stage is deterministic. The answer is based only on which sentences remain in the page.

Rule 1

Cross-outs remove events

A crossed-out sentence no longer happens. Restore it by clicking it again.

Rule 2

Required facts must remain

Some sentences are necessary for the successful ending. Removing them makes the story fail even if they sound dangerous.

Rule 3

Causes matter more than consequences

Later levels hide a failure several sentences after its actual cause. Trace the chain backward.

Rule 4

No answer meter

The UI does not become green, safe, stable, warmer, or colder while you edit. Only Check Survival judges the page.

Rule 5

Bad checks have consequences

When you check an incorrect page, the aftermath scene shows what your version caused.

Rule 6

Three-star par

A correct page always clears the level. Clean solutions at or under par earn the maximum three stars.

Rule 7

World progression

Levels unlock in order. Finishing level 10 unlocks the next world and triggers its world-route animation.
