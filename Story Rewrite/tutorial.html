Tutorial

How the book works

The game is not about deleting every scary sentence. You are trying to create the smallest version of the story where the objective is true and the required characters still survive.

Step 1

Cross out an event

Click a sentence to remove that event from the active story. Click it again to restore it.

The creature hears the door slam and turns toward the house.
Step 2

Read the objective

The objective, threat, and survival rule all matter. A route can look safer while still making the ending impossible.

Step 3

The art will not solve it

The picture-book illustration changes only after a failed check. There is no stable/unstable color clue while you are guessing.

Step 4

Failed checks show the aftermath

If the story is wrong, the right-hand page shows the bad thing that happens afterward. Fix your redactions and try again.

Step 5

Stars reward clean edits

Three stars means you solved the story at par without deleting unnecessary lines.

Step 6

Worlds unlock through the map

Finish level 10 of a world, watch its chapter animation, then the ink dots travel to the next world and light it up.
