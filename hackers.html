Investigation rules

Safeguards applied across all developer tiers.

Lower-tier developers can read investigation records but cannot change case status or carry out enforcement. Moderators can submit reports and request higher-level action. Top-tier moderators can change investigation status and review action requests, with StoryBot checks still applied. Only Darius56 can appoint or remove developer roles.

This page does not provide player screen, camera, microphone, or private-device monitoring. Evidence must be deliberately recorded as part of a legitimate investigation, and calls require the player's manual acceptance.

A reported account is not automatically guilty. Use evidence, context, proportional decisions, and the audit trail.
