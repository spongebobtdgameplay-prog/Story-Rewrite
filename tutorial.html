Tutorial
Single player and multiplayer guides.
How to Play

Learn the rewrite, then learn the room

Single Player teaches the story puzzle. Multiplayer teaches codes, voting, team lives, host controls, chat, late joins, and StoryBot.

Single Player

Rewrite the story without breaking the ending

Cross out the causes of failure while preserving everything the successful outcome still needs.

Step 1

Cross out an event

Click or tap a sentence to remove that event from the active story. Select it again to restore it.

The creature hears the door slam and turns toward the house.
Step 2

Read the briefing

Objective is what must happen. Threat is the danger. Must survive is what your successful rewrite cannot erase.

Step 3

Trace causes backward

The disaster may happen several sentences after the real mistake. Remove the cause, not only the final consequence.

Step 4

Do not erase useful facts

Some dangerous-looking lines are still required. A rewrite can look safer and still fail because the ending lost something it needed.

Step 5

Check Survival when ready

The editor does not reveal correct answers while you work. Press Check Survival when you think the route is safe.

Step 6

Lives are limited

A failed check costs one life and shows the aftermath your rewrite caused. Use that information to repair the story.

Step 7

Cleaner solutions earn more stars

A correct solution clears the stage. Three stars means you stayed at or under par without removing unnecessary events.

Step 8

Move through the chapter map

Stages unlock in order. Completing the chapter opens the next route and refills your chapter lives.

Multiplayer

Solve one story as a team

Up to five players share the room, votes, story state, and team lives. The host controls the session.

Room codes

Create or join a private room

The host creates a six-character code and sends it to the group. Other players enter that code from Multiplayer.

Lobby

Ready up before starting

Players mark themselves Ready. The host starts the story once the group is prepared.

Voting

Sentence edits use majority votes

Tap a sentence to vote for crossing it out. The edit becomes active when it reaches the room's current majority threshold.

Team survival

Everyone shares the same lives

Failed survival checks remove a team life for the whole room. Only the host submits the final survival check.

Late joins

Started rooms require host approval

If a new player enters the code after the game started, the host receives an Allow or Deny request. Returning members can reconnect normally.

Host controls

The host moderates the room

The host can approve late joins, kick players, and disable or restore an individual player's room chat.

Group chat

Chat is limited and moderated

Messages have a length limit and server-side spam protection. Chat can be hidden during gameplay, and filtered messages are handled on the backend.

AI teammate

Ask StoryBot from the room

Mention @StoryBot followed by your question. StoryBot receives the live stage, room state, votes, and recent chat so it can answer using what is actually happening.

Example: @StoryBot which event looks like the cause of the current threat?
Mobile

Mobile gets dedicated controls

On touch devices, use the bottom Map, Restore, Check, and Chat controls. Sentence voting still works by tapping the sentence directly.
