How to Play

Rewrite the story without breaking the ending

Each stage gives you a dangerous chain of events. Your job is to cross out the causes that make the story fail while keeping every event the successful ending still needs.

Step 1

Cross out an event

Click a sentence to remove that event from the active story. Click it again to restore it. Try the sentence below.

The creature hears the door slam and turns toward the house.
Step 2

Read the stage briefing

Objective tells you what must happen. Threat tells you what can ruin it. Must survive tells you what the successful version of the story cannot lose.

Step 3

Do not delete everything dangerous

Some scary-looking sentences are still required. If you erase a fact the ending depends on, the story fails even if the page looks safer.

Step 4

Find the cause, not just the consequence

Later stages hide the real mistake several sentences before the disaster. Trace the chain backward and remove the event that causes the bad outcome.

Step 5

There are no answer hints while editing

Crossing out a sentence will not turn the interface green or red. The game does not tell you whether an edit is correct until you press Check Survival.

Step 6

A failed check costs a life

If your rewrite fails, the aftermath shows what your version caused and you lose one life. Use that outcome as information, repair the page, and try again.

Step 7

Clean solutions earn more stars

Any correct rewrite clears the level. Three stars means you solved it at or under par without erasing unnecessary events. Extra removals can reduce the star score.

Step 8

Progress through the chapter map

Stages unlock in order. Finish level 10 to complete the chapter, refill your lives, and open the route into the next world.
